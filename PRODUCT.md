# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The platform serves laboratory administrators, system administrators, teachers, and maintenance staff in a school laboratory operations context. Users work inside a task-focused management console where they need to maintain laboratory resources, coordinate teaching schedules and reservations, monitor environments, handle repair workflows, and review operational data.

## Product Purpose

Zhiyun Laboratory is a data-driven collaborative laboratory management platform. It connects laboratory resources, teaching schedules, reservations, equipment status, environment preparation, repair handling, and statistical analysis into a complete operations loop. Success means administrators can understand resource status quickly, teachers can coordinate course needs, maintenance staff can close repair tasks, and management can make decisions from reliable operational data.

## Brand Personality

Calm, reliable, organized. The interface should feel like a serious campus operations tool: clear, efficient, and trustworthy, with enough visual hierarchy to help users understand workflows without decorative noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid a menu that reads like a loose list of features. Avoid marketing-style hero layouts, decorative cards, excessive gradients, vague labels such as "extensions" without business meaning, and module names that mix resources, processes, monitoring, and analysis in the same group.

## Design Principles

1. Organize by laboratory operations flow, not by implementation detail.
2. Keep navigation labels concrete, formal, and presentation-ready for graduation defense.
3. Make the sidebar explain the system: resource setup, teaching coordination, environment operation, repair handling, and data intelligence.
4. Preserve familiar admin UI patterns so users can act without relearning controls.
5. Use restrained visual treatment and let hierarchy, naming, and grouping carry clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable Chinese labels, predictable keyboard and mouse interactions, and adequate contrast for long administrative sessions. Motion should be minimal and state-driven. Navigation should remain usable when collapsed or on smaller screens.
